Stardew Valley Sprinkler FAQs

How many crops does an Iridium Sprinkler water?

An Iridium Sprinkler waters 24 crops in a 5x5 area (the sprinkler sits in the center tile, which stays dry). That is the most efficient sprinkler in Stardew Valley and covers an entire 5x5 crop block.

How many crops does a Quality Sprinkler water?

A Quality Sprinkler waters 8 crops in a 3x3 area around it. Unlocked at Farming level 6, it needs 1 Iron Bar, 1 Gold Bar and 1 Refined Quartz to craft, and it is the best mid-game option.

What is the best sprinkler layout for a 5x5 farm?

For a 5x5 farm plot, one Iridium Sprinkler in the center covers all 24 surrounding tiles. For larger fields, place Iridium Sprinklers every 5 tiles (a 5x5 grid) so every crop is watered with zero gaps.

Can sprinklers water seeds planted on the same day?

No. Sprinklers water crops at the start of the day, so seeds planted after that watering pass will not be watered until the next morning. Plan planting in the evening or before 2am sleep so your new seeds get watered the next day.

When can I unlock the Basic Sprinkler?

The Basic Sprinkler unlocks at Farming level 2 and costs 1 Copper Bar and 1 Iron Bar. It waters 4 tiles directly up, down, left and right, making it useful only for a very small early-game garden.
