Trust and scope

What this MVP knows, and what it does not.

📋

Versioned reference data

The bundle groups, dateable-villager gift examples, and crop presets are tied to Stardew Valley 1.6.15 PC references and verified on 2026-08-11. They remain a focused subset, not a complete automatically updated wiki.

📄

Source before scale

Each interactive record carries a source link, version boundary, and verification date. Remixed bundles, platform differences, and preference states outside the current scope are intentionally excluded.

🔒

Local by default

Planner and checklist state is stored locally. No save file upload, account, or remote farm data is part of this MVP.

Questions

Stardew Valley
Planner FAQ

Short answers for the tool's current scope. Production pages should expand these with versioned data and route-specific examples.

What does this Stardew Valley Planner do?

It provides a farm layout grid, a community center bundle checklist, a gift lookup, and a crop profit calculator — all in one workspace for planning your run.

Does the farm planner know every in-game collision rule?

No. The grid is a sketch tool. Confirm exact footprints and collision rules in-game before building.

Is the bundle checklist complete?

It covers the standard 1.6.15 bundles. Mixed variants and platform differences are not included in this first version.

Can I save my Stardew Valley farm layout?

Yes. Save draft stores your plan name and tile map in your browser. Export JSON gives you a portable backup.

How does the crop calculator work?

Enter seed cost, sell price, growth days, harvest count, and extra cost. Net profit = (revenue − seed cost − extra cost) / growth days.

Does Valley Ledger belong to Stardew Valley?

No. This is an unofficial fan resource built from open-source game data. Not affiliated with ConcernedApe.
