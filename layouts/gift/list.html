Why gift lookup belongs next to the planner

Gift timing is date-based friendship. It tells you which items are available in your current farm, which gifts you can harvest from your own crops, and which items can be bought at multiple shops.

Limits you should know

This first version covers universal loved gifts and profession bonuses. Mixed bundles and platform differences are not included. Check the full gift guide for exact heart requirements and modded crops.
