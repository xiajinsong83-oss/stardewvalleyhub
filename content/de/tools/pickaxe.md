The **Pickaxe** is a tool in Stardew Valley.

Break rocks and mine ore. Higher upgrades break tougher rocks and allow picking up placed items.

**Upgrades**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Level | Cost (g) | Material | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| basic | - | -  | Breaks small rocks. Effective in mine floors 1–39. |
| copper | 2000 | Copper Bar 5 | Breaks all rocks in mine floors 1–39 in one hit. |
| steel | 5000 | Iron Bar 5 | Breaks farm boulders. Effective in mine floors 40–79. |
| gold | 10000 | Gold Bar 5 | Breaks meteorites. Effective in mine floors 80–120. |
| iridium | 25000 | Iridium Bar 5 | Breaks Quarry and Skull Cavern rocks in one hit. |
</div>

**Enchantable:** Yes

## Tips

- Upgrade tools at Clint's Blacksmith with bars and gold.
- Higher levels allow charged actions (hoe, watering can).
