The **Pan** is a tool in Stardew Valley.

Gather ore, gems, and other items from shimmering spots in water. Higher upgrades increase yield and chance for special items.

**Upgrades**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Level | Cost (g) | Material | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| copper | - | -  | Yields 3–9 ore from shimmering water spots. |
| steel | 5000 | Iron Bar 5 | Yields 4–10 ore. Up to 2 special items per use. |
| gold | 10000 | Gold Bar 5 | Yields 5–11 ore. Up to 3 special items per use. |
| iridium | 25000 | Iridium Bar 5 | Yields 6–12 ore. Up to 4 special items per use. |
</div>

**Enchantable:** Yes

## Tips

- Upgrade tools at Clint's Blacksmith with bars and gold.
- Higher levels allow charged actions (hoe, watering can).
