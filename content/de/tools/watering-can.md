The **Watering Can** is a tool in Stardew Valley.

Water crops each day. Higher upgrades increase water capacity and allow watering multiple tiles at once when charged.

**Upgrades**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Level | Cost (g) | Material | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| basic | - | -  | 40 water charges. Waters 1 tile. |
| copper | 2000 | Copper Bar 5 | 55 water charges. Waters 3 tiles in a line when charged. |
| steel | 5000 | Iron Bar 5 | 70 water charges. Waters 5 tiles in a line when charged. |
| gold | 10000 | Gold Bar 5 | 85 water charges. Waters a 3×3 area when charged. |
| iridium | 25000 | Iridium Bar 5 | 100 water charges. Waters a 6×3 area when charged. |
</div>

**Enchantable:** Yes

## Tips

- Upgrade tools at Clint's Blacksmith with bars and gold.
- Higher levels allow charged actions (hoe, watering can).
