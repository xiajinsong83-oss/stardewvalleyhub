The **Axe** is a tool in Stardew Valley.

Chop trees for wood and sap. Higher upgrades reduce hits required and unlock chopping hardwood stumps and logs.

**Upgrades**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Level | Cost (g) | Material | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| basic | - | -  | 10 hits to fell a tree, 5 hits to chop a stump. |
| copper | 2000 | Copper Bar 5 | 8 hits to fell a tree, 4 hits to chop a stump. |
| steel | 5000 | Iron Bar 5 | 6 hits to fell a tree, 3 hits to chop a stump. Can chop large stumps f |
| gold | 10000 | Gold Bar 5 | 4 hits to fell a tree, 2 hits to chop a stump. |
| iridium | 25000 | Iridium Bar 5 | 2 hits to fell a tree, 1 hit to chop a stump. |
</div>

**Enchantable:** Yes

## Tips

- Upgrade tools at Clint's Blacksmith with bars and gold.
- Higher levels allow charged actions (hoe, watering can).
