The **Bone Flute** is a artifact that can be donated to the Stardew Valley Museum.

**Sell price:** 100g

**Found in:** Forest (0.7%), Mountain (0.7%), Town (0.4%), Fishing Treasure Chest, Artifact Trove (3.6%)

**Donation note:** Donating grants a Flute Block

## Tips

- Donate the first of each item, then sell duplicates.
- Minerals sell for more with the Gemologist profession when applicable.
