The **Dwarf Scroll IV** is a artifact that can be donated to the Stardew Valley Museum.

**Sell price:** 1g

**Found in:** Most monsters (0.1%), Tilling in Mines (floor 80+) (0.2%)

**Donation note:** One of four scrolls; donating all four unlocks Dwarvish Translation Guide

## Tips

- Donate the first of each item, then sell duplicates.
- Minerals sell for more with the Gemologist profession when applicable.
