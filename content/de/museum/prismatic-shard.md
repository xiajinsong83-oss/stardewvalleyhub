The **Prismatic Shard** is a mineral that can be donated to the Stardew Valley Museum.

**Sell price:** 2000g

**Found in:** Iridium Node (4%), Mystic Stone (25%), Omni Geode (0.4%), Shadow creatures, Fishing Treasure Chest

**Donation note:** No extra donation note is recorded in the open data.

## Tips

- Donate the first of each item, then sell duplicates.
- Minerals sell for more with the Gemologist profession when applicable.
