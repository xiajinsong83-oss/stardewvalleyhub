The **Chewing Stick** is a artifact that can be donated to the Stardew Valley Museum.

**Sell price:** 50g

**Found in:** Mountain (1.5%), Forest (1.5%), Town (0.8%), Fishing Treasure Chest, Duggy drop (2%), Artifact Trove (3.6%)

**Donation note:** No extra donation note is recorded in the open data.

## Tips

- Donate the first of each item, then sell duplicates.
- Minerals sell for more with the Gemologist profession when applicable.
