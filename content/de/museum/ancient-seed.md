The **Ancient Seed** is a artifact that can be donated to the Stardew Valley Museum.

**Sell price:** 5g

**Found in:** Forest (0.7%), Mountain (0.7%), Fishing Treasure Chest, Bug, Cave Fly, Grub drop (0.5%), Artifact Trove (3.6%)

**Donation note:** Donating grants Ancient Seed crafting recipe and 1 Ancient Seeds pack

## Tips

- Donate the first of each item, then sell duplicates.
- Minerals sell for more with the Gemologist profession when applicable.
