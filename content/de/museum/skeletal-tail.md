The **Skeletal Tail** is a artifact that can be donated to the Stardew Valley Museum.

**Sell price:** 100g

**Found in:** Tilling in Mines/Skull Cavern, Fishing Treasure Chest (3.1-3.4%), Bone Nodes (0.8%)

**Donation note:** Part of the Sloth Skeleton display in the museum

## Tips

- Donate the first of each item, then sell duplicates.
- Minerals sell for more with the Gemologist profession when applicable.
