The **Rabbit** is a farm animal.

A fluffy coop animal that sheds Wool every few days. Very happy rabbits may drop a lucky Rabbit's Foot.

**Housing:** Deluxe Coop · **Purchase:** 4000g · **Sell:** 8000g

**Produce:** Wool (sells for 340g) · every 4 day(s)

## Tips

- Feed animals daily (hay in the silo or grass outside).
- Deluxe produce unlocks with the Coopmaster/Shepherd profession and high friendship.
