The **Golden Chicken** is a farm animal.

A rare golden chicken hatched from a Golden Egg. Produces Golden Eggs daily.

**Housing:** Coop · **Purchase:** Noneg · **Sell:** 800g

**Produce:** Golden Egg (sells for 500g) · every 1 day(s)

## Tips

- Feed animals daily (hay in the silo or grass outside).
- Deluxe produce unlocks with the Coopmaster/Shepherd profession and high friendship.
