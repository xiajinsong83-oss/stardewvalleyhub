The **Pig** is a farm animal.

Forages for Truffles outside each day in clear weather. Cannot produce in winter.

**Housing:** Deluxe Barn · **Purchase:** 8000g · **Sell:** 16000g

**Produce:** Truffle (sells for 625g) · every 1 day(s)

## Tips

- Feed animals daily (hay in the silo or grass outside).
- Deluxe produce unlocks with the Coopmaster/Shepherd profession and high friendship.
