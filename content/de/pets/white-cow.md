The **White Cow** is a farm animal.

A dairy cow that produces milk daily. Must be milked with a Milk Pail.

**Housing:** Barn · **Purchase:** 750g · **Sell:** 1500g

**Produce:** Milk (sells for 125g) · every 1 day(s)

## Tips

- Feed animals daily (hay in the silo or grass outside).
- Deluxe produce unlocks with the Coopmaster/Shepherd profession and high friendship.
