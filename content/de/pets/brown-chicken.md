The **Brown Chicken** is a farm animal.

A common farm bird with brown plumage. Lays brown eggs each morning.

**Housing:** Coop · **Purchase:** Noneg · **Sell:** 800g

**Produce:** Brown Egg (sells for 50g) · every 1 day(s)

## Tips

- Feed animals daily (hay in the silo or grass outside).
