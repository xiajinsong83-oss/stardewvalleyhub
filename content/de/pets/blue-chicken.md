The **Blue Chicken** is a farm animal.

A rare blue-feathered chicken unlocked through a deep friendship with Shane. Produces white eggs daily.

**Housing:** Coop · **Purchase:** Noneg · **Sell:** 800g

**Produce:** Egg (sells for 50g) · every 1 day(s)

## Tips

- Feed animals daily (hay in the silo or grass outside).
- Deluxe produce unlocks with the Coopmaster/Shepherd profession and high friendship.
