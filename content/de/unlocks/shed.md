The **Shed** is a building on your farm.

An empty building where you can place crafting machines, kegs, preserves jars, etc.

**Cost:** 15000g · **Build time:** 2 days · **Builder:** Robin

**Materials**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Material | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Wood | 300 |
</div>

**Upgrade from:** None · **Animal capacity:** - · **Magical:** No

## Tips

- Buildings are placed by Robin (or the Wizard for magical buildings).
- Upgrading unlocks more animals and processing options.
