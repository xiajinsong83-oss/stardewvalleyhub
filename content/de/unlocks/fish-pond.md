The **Fish Pond** is a building on your farm.

Place fish in a pond to raise them. Produces roe and other items.

**Cost:** 5000g · **Build time:** 2 days · **Builder:** Robin

**Materials**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Material | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Stone | 200 |
| Seaweed | 5 |
| Green Algae | 5 |
</div>

**Upgrade from:** None · **Animal capacity:** - · **Magical:** No

## Tips

- Buildings are placed by Robin (or the Wizard for magical buildings).
- Upgrading unlocks more animals and processing options.
