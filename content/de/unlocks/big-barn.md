The **Big Barn** is a building on your farm.

An upgraded barn with more space. Houses up to 8 animals including sheep and pigs.

**Cost:** 12000g · **Build time:** 2 days · **Builder:** Robin

**Materials**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Material | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Wood | 450 |
| Stone | 200 |
</div>

**Upgrade from:** barn · **Animal capacity:** 8 · **Magical:** No

## Tips

- Buildings are placed by Robin (or the Wizard for magical buildings).
- Upgrading unlocks more animals and processing options.
