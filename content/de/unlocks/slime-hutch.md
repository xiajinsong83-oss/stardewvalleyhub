The **Slime Hutch** is a building on your farm.

Raise slimes in this building. Fill the water troughs and collect slime balls.

**Cost:** 10000g · **Build time:** 2 days · **Builder:** Robin

**Materials**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Material | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Stone | 500 |
| Refined Quartz | 10 |
| Iridium Bar | 1 |
</div>

**Upgrade from:** None · **Animal capacity:** 20 · **Magical:** No

## Tips

- Buildings are placed by Robin (or the Wizard for magical buildings).
- Upgrading unlocks more animals and processing options.
