The **Junimo Hut** is a building on your farm.

Junimos will harvest crops within a certain radius of the hut. Place it near your crops.

**Cost:** 20000g · **Build time:** 0 days · **Builder:** Wizard

**Materials**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Material | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Stone | 200 |
| Starfruit | 9 |
| Fiber | 100 |
</div>

**Upgrade from:** None · **Animal capacity:** - · **Magical:** Yes

## Tips

- Buildings are placed by Robin (or the Wizard for magical buildings).
- Upgrading unlocks more animals and processing options.
