The Community Center has these rooms: **Abandoned Joja Mart, Boiler Room, Bulletin Board, Crafts Room, Fish Tank, Pantry**.

Completing a room unlocks its reward: the Crafts Room unlocks bridge repair, the Pantry unlocks the greenhouse, the Boiler Room unlocks minecarts, the Fish Tank unlocks the mine elevator, and the Bulletin Board unlocks the movie theater (with the Vault). Completing all bundles unlocks the Junimo Hut ceremony.
