Complete all six Pantry bundles (Spring Crops, Summer Crops, Fall Crops, Quality Crops, Animal, Artisan). The reward is the greenhouse, which lets you grow crops in any season.

Items for the Pantry include parsnips, melons, pumpkins, artisan goods and animal products.
