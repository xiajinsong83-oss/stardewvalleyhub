**Gifts for George** — requested by **Evelyn**

George thinks no one in town cares about him. Let's show him that isn't true.

**Timeframe:** 28 days

**Requirements:** Collect and place 12 Leeks onto Evelyn's stove

**Rewards:** 2,000g, Coffee Maker, cutscene at George's house

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
