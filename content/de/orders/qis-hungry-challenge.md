**Qi's Hungry Challenge** — requested by **Qi**

Your task is to make it to level 100 in the Skull Cavern without consuming any food or drink.

**Timeframe:** 7 days

**Requirements:** Reach floor 100 of Skull Cavern without consuming food or drink (Staircases allowed)

**Rewards:** 25 Qi Gems

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
