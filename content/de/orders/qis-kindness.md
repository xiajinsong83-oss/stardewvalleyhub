**Qi's Kindness** — requested by **Qi**

Give 50 loved gifts in one week.

**Timeframe:** 7 days

**Requirements:** Give 50 loved gifts to villagers within one week

**Rewards:** 40 Qi Gems

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
