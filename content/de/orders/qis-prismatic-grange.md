**Qi's Prismatic Grange** — requested by **Qi**

Find 100 each of red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple items and place them in my collection box.

**Timeframe:** 14 days

**Requirements:** Place 100 each of red, orange, yellow, green, blue, and purple items in Qi's collection box (600 items total)

**Rewards:** 35 Qi Gems

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
