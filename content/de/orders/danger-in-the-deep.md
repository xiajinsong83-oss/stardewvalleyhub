**Danger In The Deep** — requested by **Qi**

The mine elevator system has been reset, and new dangers have emerged. Reach the bottom again.

**Timeframe:** 7 days

**Requirements:** Reach the bottom of the Mines (Staircases allowed); resets unlocked Adventurer's Guild gear

**Rewards:** 50 Qi Gems

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
