**Biome Balance** — requested by **Demetrius**

For unknown reasons, the local population of fish has grown to an unsustainable level. A broader variety is needed.

**Timeframe:** 7 days

**Requirements:** Catch 20 of: River Fish, Ocean Fish, or Lake Fish

**Rewards:** 1,500g, Farm Computer recipe

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
