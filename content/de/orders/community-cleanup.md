**Community Cleanup** — requested by **Linus**

I have an idea. There's a lot of trash in the water around town. Want to help clean it up?

**Timeframe:** 7 days

**Requirements:** Gather 20 trash items and dump them in the bin at the train platform

**Rewards:** 500g; 250 Friendship points with Linus; Fiber Seeds recipe; cutscene at Mountain Lake

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
