**Gus' Famous Omelet** — requested by **Gus**

I've got the urge to make my famous giant omelet. I'll need a lot of eggs.

**Timeframe:** 14 days

**Requirements:** Place 24 Eggs into Stardrop Saloon fridge

**Rewards:** 3,000g; Mini-Fridge (first time); Giant Omelet cutscene (first time)

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
