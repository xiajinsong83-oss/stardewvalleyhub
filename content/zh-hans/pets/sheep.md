The **Sheep** is a farm animal.

A barn animal that grows a fleece every three days. Must be sheared with Shears. Very friendly sheep grow wool faster.

**Housing:** Deluxe Barn · **Purchase:** 4000g · **Sell:** 8000g

**Produce:** Wool (sells for 340g) · every 3 day(s)

## Tips

- Feed animals daily (hay in the silo or grass outside).
- Deluxe produce unlocks with the Coopmaster/Shepherd profession and high friendship.
