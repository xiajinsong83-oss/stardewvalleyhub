The **Dinosaur** is a farm animal.

A prehistoric creature hatched from a Dinosaur Egg found in the mines. Lays another Dinosaur Egg each week.

**Housing:** Coop · **Purchase:** Noneg · **Sell:** 1000g

**Produce:** Dinosaur Egg (sells for 350g) · every 7 day(s)

## Tips

- Feed animals daily (hay in the silo or grass outside).
- Deluxe produce unlocks with the Coopmaster/Shepherd profession and high friendship.
