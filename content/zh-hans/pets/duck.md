The **Duck** is a farm animal.

A waterfowl that lays a Duck Egg every other day. Happy ducks occasionally drop a valuable Duck Feather.

**Housing:** Big Coop · **Purchase:** 600g · **Sell:** 1200g

**Produce:** Duck Egg (sells for 95g) · every 2 day(s)

## Tips

- Feed animals daily (hay in the silo or grass outside).
- Deluxe produce unlocks with the Coopmaster/Shepherd profession and high friendship.
