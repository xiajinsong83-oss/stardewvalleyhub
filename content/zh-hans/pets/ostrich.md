The **Ostrich** is a farm animal.

A large bird hatched from an Ostrich Egg found on Ginger Island. Produces a massive egg every seven days.

**Housing:** Barn · **Purchase:** Noneg · **Sell:** 16000g

**Produce:** Ostrich Egg (sells for 600g) · every 7 day(s)

## Tips

- Feed animals daily (hay in the silo or grass outside).
- Deluxe produce unlocks with the Coopmaster/Shepherd profession and high friendship.
