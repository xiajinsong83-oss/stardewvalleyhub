The **Cat** is a pet in Stardew Valley.

Pets give friendship over time; water them daily and they follow you around the farm.

**Variants recorded:** 5

## Tips

- Pets do not need feeding, only affection (petting/watering).
- A fully befriended pet can be made your farm's companion.
