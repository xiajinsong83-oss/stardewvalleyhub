The **Goat** is a farm animal.

A barn animal that produces Goat Milk every other day. Must be milked with a Milk Pail.

**Housing:** Big Barn · **Purchase:** 2000g · **Sell:** 4000g

**Produce:** Goat Milk (sells for 225g) · every 2 day(s)

## Tips

- Feed animals daily (hay in the silo or grass outside).
- Deluxe produce unlocks with the Coopmaster/Shepherd profession and high friendship.
