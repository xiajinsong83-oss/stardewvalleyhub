The **Well** is a building on your farm.

Place a well to refill your watering can without having to travel to a pond or river.

**Cost:** 1000g · **Build time:** 2 days · **Builder:** Robin

**Materials**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Material | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Stone | 75 |
</div>

**Upgrade from:** None · **Animal capacity:** - · **Magical:** No

## Tips

- Buildings are placed by Robin (or the Wizard for magical buildings).
- Upgrading unlocks more animals and processing options.
