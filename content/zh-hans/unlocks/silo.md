The **Silo** is a building on your farm.

Stores hay from cut grass. Capacity: 240 hay per silo.

**Cost:** 100g · **Build time:** 2 days · **Builder:** Robin

**Materials**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Material | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Stone | 100 |
| Clay | 10 |
| Copper Bar | 5 |
</div>

**Upgrade from:** None · **Animal capacity:** - · **Magical:** No

## Tips

- Buildings are placed by Robin (or the Wizard for magical buildings).
- Upgrading unlocks more animals and processing options.
