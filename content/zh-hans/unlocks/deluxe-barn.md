The **Deluxe Barn** is a building on your farm.

The best barn. Houses up to 12 animals including ostriches. Has an auto-feed system.

**Cost:** 25000g · **Build time:** 2 days · **Builder:** Robin

**Materials**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Material | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Wood | 550 |
| Stone | 300 |
</div>

**Upgrade from:** big-barn · **Animal capacity:** 12 · **Magical:** No

## Tips

- Buildings are placed by Robin (or the Wizard for magical buildings).
- Upgrading unlocks more animals and processing options.
