The **Barn** is a building on your farm.

A building that houses cows and goats. Feed them with hay from a silo.

**Cost:** 6000g · **Build time:** 3 days · **Builder:** Robin

**Materials**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Material | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Wood | 350 |
| Stone | 150 |
</div>

**Upgrade from:** None · **Animal capacity:** 4 · **Magical:** No

## Tips

- Buildings are placed by Robin (or the Wizard for magical buildings).
- Upgrading unlocks more animals and processing options.
