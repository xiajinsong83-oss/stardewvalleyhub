The **Big Coop** is a building on your farm.

An upgraded coop with more space. Houses up to 8 animals including ducks and dinosaurs. Includes an incubator.

**Cost:** 10000g · **Build time:** 2 days · **Builder:** Robin

**Materials**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Material | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Wood | 400 |
| Stone | 150 |
</div>

**Upgrade from:** coop · **Animal capacity:** 8 · **Magical:** No

## Tips

- Buildings are placed by Robin (or the Wizard for magical buildings).
- Upgrading unlocks more animals and processing options.
