The **Mill** is a building on your farm.

Place crops inside to make Flour, Sugar, Rice, and Wheat Flour.

**Cost:** 2500g · **Build time:** 2 days · **Builder:** Robin

**Materials**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Material | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Wood | 150 |
| Stone | 50 |
| Cloth | 4 |
</div>

**Upgrade from:** None · **Animal capacity:** - · **Magical:** No

## Tips

- Buildings are placed by Robin (or the Wizard for magical buildings).
- Upgrading unlocks more animals and processing options.
