**Qi's Crop** — requested by **Qi**

I've hidden Qi Beans throughout the world. Find them, grow them, propagate them, and ship the results.

**Timeframe:** 28 days

**Requirements:** Ship 500 Qi Fruit using the Shipping Bin

**Rewards:** 100 Qi Gems

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
