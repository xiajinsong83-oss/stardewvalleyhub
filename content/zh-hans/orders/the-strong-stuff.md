**The Strong Stuff** — requested by **Qi**

Ship quality items worth a total of at least 100,000g.

**Timeframe:** 14 days

**Requirements:** Ship items with quality stars worth a total of at least 100,000g

**Rewards:** 25 Qi Gems

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
