**Aquatic Overpopulation** — requested by **Demetrius**

For unknown reasons, the local population of fish has grown to an unsustainable level. Can you help restore balance?

**Timeframe:** 7 days

**Requirements:** Catch 10 seasonal fish

**Rewards:** Gold (fish sell value), Farm Computer recipe

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
