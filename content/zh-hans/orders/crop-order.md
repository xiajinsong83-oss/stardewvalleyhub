**Crop Order** — requested by **Lewis**

Crops are in high demand this year. Ship a large batch of a seasonal crop.

**Timeframe:** 28 days

**Requirements:** Ship 100 of a seasonal crop

**Rewards:** Gold (50% base quality crop price); Mini-Shipping Bin by mail (first time)

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
