**Pierre's Prime Produce** — requested by **Pierre**

For an upcoming promotion, I'm thinking of offering some high-quality vegetables.

**Timeframe:** 28 days

**Requirements:** Dump 25 Gold-quality vegetables in the bin at General Store

**Rewards:** 2,500g, cutscene at General Store, Mini-Shipping Bin by mail

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
