**Fragments of the Past** — requested by **Gunther**

Calling all amateur paleontologists! Bring any bone-related artifacts or items to the Museum.

**Timeframe:** 7 days

**Requirements:** Collect 100 bone items and deposit them in the Museum box

**Rewards:** 3,500g, Bone Mill recipe

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
