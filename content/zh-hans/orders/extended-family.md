**Extended Family** — requested by **Qi**

Family members of the legendary fish have returned to the valley. Catch them all.

**Timeframe:** 3 days

**Requirements:** Catch Ms. Angler, Glacierfish Jr., Son of Crimsonfish, Radioactive Carp, and Legend II

**Rewards:** 20 Qi Gems

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
