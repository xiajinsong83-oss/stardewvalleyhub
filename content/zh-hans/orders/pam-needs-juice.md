**Pam Needs Juice** — requested by **Pam**

Heard there's a spirit made out of potatoes. Think you could bring me some potato juice?

**Timeframe:** 14 days

**Requirements:** Place 12 Potato Juice in Pam's kitchen

**Rewards:** 3,000g; 250 Friendship points with Pam; Potato Juice cutscene; F.I.B.S. TV channel

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
