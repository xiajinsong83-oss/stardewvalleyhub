**Robin's Resource Rush** — requested by **Robin**

I'm putting on a little promotion, just for fun. Whoever brings me the most resources wins a prize.

**Timeframe:** 7 days

**Requirements:** Collect 1,000 of: Wood or Stone

**Rewards:** 2,500g, Stone Chest recipe

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
