**Cave Patrol** — requested by **Clint**

The number of monsters in the local caves have made mining dangerous. Would you help thin out their numbers?

**Timeframe:** 7 days

**Requirements:** Slay 50 of: Bats, Dust Sprites, Skeletons, or Grubs

**Rewards:** 6,000g; Geode Crusher recipe (first time); Explosives and food by letter (subsequent times)

## Tips

- Check the special order board after completing the Community Center (or Joja route).
- Gather required items before the deadline; failed orders can reappear.
