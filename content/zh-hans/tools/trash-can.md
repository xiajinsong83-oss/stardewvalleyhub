The **Trash Can** is a tool in Stardew Valley.

Discard items from your inventory. Higher upgrades refund a percentage of the item's sell value.

**Upgrades**

<div class="table-wrap">

| Level | Cost (g) | Material | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| basic | - | -  | Discards items. No sell value refunded. |
| copper | 1000 | Copper Bar 5 | Refunds 15% of an item's sell value when trashed. |
| steel | 2500 | Iron Bar 5 | Refunds 30% of an item's sell value when trashed. |
| gold | 5000 | Gold Bar 5 | Refunds 45% of an item's sell value when trashed. |
| iridium | 12500 | Iridium Bar 5 | Refunds 60% of an item's sell value when trashed. |
</div>

**Enchantable:** No

## Tips

- Upgrade tools at Clint's Blacksmith with bars and gold.
- Higher levels allow charged actions (hoe, watering can).
