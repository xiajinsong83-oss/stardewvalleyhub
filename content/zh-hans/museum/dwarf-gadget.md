The **Dwarf Gadget** is a artifact that can be donated to the Stardew Valley Museum.

**Sell price:** 200g

**Found in:** Tilling in Mines (floor 40-79) (0.1%), Magma Geode (4%), Omni Geode (1%), Artifact Trove (3.6%)

**Donation note:** No extra donation note is recorded in the open data.

## Tips

- Donate the first of each item, then sell duplicates.
- Minerals sell for more with the Gemologist profession when applicable.
