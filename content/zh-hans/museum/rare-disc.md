The **Rare Disc** is a artifact that can be donated to the Stardew Valley Museum.

**Sell price:** 300g

**Found in:** Fishing Treasure Chest, Most Bats (0.1%), Shadow Brute, Shadow Shaman, Spider drop (0.3%), Artifact Trove (3.6%)

**Donation note:** No extra donation note is recorded in the open data.

## Tips

- Donate the first of each item, then sell duplicates.
- Minerals sell for more with the Gemologist profession when applicable.
