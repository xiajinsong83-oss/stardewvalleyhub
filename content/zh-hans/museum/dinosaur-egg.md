The **Dinosaur Egg** is a artifact that can be donated to the Stardew Valley Museum.

**Sell price:** 350g

**Found in:** Mountain (0.6%), Fishing Treasure Chest, Prehistoric floors in Skull Cavern, Pepper Rex drop (10%)

**Donation note:** Can be hatched in an Incubator to obtain a Dinosaur

## Tips

- Donate the first of each item, then sell duplicates.
- Minerals sell for more with the Gemologist profession when applicable.
