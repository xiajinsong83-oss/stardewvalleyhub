The **Wild Medicine Bundle** is a bundle in the **Crafts Room** room of the Community Center.

**Items required:** 1 of 4

<div class="table-wrap">

| Item | Quantity |
| --- | --- |
| Purple Mushroom | 5 |
| Fiddlehead Fern | 5 |
| White Algae | 5 |
| Hops | 5 |
</div>

**Reward:** 2x Cookout Kit

## Tips

- Collect required items across all seasons; check each item's source before the season ends.
- Completing all bundles in a room unlocks room-specific rewards (e.g. greenhouse, quarry, minecarts).
