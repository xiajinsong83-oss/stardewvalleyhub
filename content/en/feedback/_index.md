## How to give useful feedback

- **Bugs:** include platform, game version (e.g. 1.6.x), whether mods are installed, and clear reproduction steps.
- **Suggestions:** be specific about the feature, where it fits in the game, and how it improves play.
- **Be respectful:** keep reports constructive; the game team reads the official forums and Discord.
