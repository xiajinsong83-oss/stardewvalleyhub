You can only be married to one villager at a time. Marriage candidates include: Abigail, Alex, Elliott, Emily, Haley, Harvey, Krobus, Leah, Maru, Penny, Sam, Sebastian, Shane.

Divorce is available at the Mayor's house (30,000g), and you can remarry afterwards.
