Legendary fish are unique, one-per-save fish with very high difficulty.

**Legendary fish:** Crimsonfish, Angler, Legend, Mutant Carp, Glacierfish.

**Legendary fish II (Ginger Island, 1.5+):** Son of Crimsonfish, Ms. Angler, Legend II, Radioactive Carp, Glacierfish Jr..

Legendary fish can only be caught once per save file (legendary II can be caught once per year). They sell for high prices and are not needed for Community Center bundles.
