Penny is a villager who lives in Pelican Town and is one of the twelve characters available to marry. She is shy and modest, and likes to cook and read books from the local library. They are one of the twelve marriage candidates.

**Birthday:** 2 Fall

**Marriage candidate:** Yes

## Tips

- Give Penny a loved gift on their birthday (2 Fall) for extra friendship points.
- Gift up to twice per week; quality and loved items add more points.
- Universal loves such as Golden Pumpkin, Magic Rock Candy and Pearl are also loved by every villager.
