Stardew Valley has no traditional boss fights, but several elite enemies act as mini-bosses: the Prismatic Slime event, the Tiger Slime on Ginger Island, and the dangerous variants that appear in the Dangerous Mines and Dangerous Skull Cavern (activated through Mr. Qi's challenge).
