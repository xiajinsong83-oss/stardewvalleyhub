## Tips

- At 20 damage per hit, bring armor with defense and food with high healing (e.g. cooked dishes from the cooking data) before engaging.
- With 700 HP, this is a sustained fight: upgrade your weapon so average damage is high, and expect roughly 7 hits with the best available weapon.
- This is a Dangerous variant: it only spawns after Mr. Qi's challenge activates dangerous mines, and it is stronger than its normal counterpart.
- Use the combat buffs from rings and footwear, and retreat when low — enemies do not chase across floor transitions.
