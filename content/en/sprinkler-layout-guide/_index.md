# Stardew Valley Sprinkler Layout Guide: Best Setups & Coverage

![Stardew Valley Sprinkler Layout](/images/farm-planner/sprinkler-layout.jpg)

Sprinklers are the backbone of efficient farming in Stardew Valley. They automatically water your crops every morning, freeing up precious time and energy for mining, fishing, foraging, and building relationships with villagers. Choosing the right sprinkler type and arranging it in an efficient layout is one of the most important early decisions you can make for your farm.

This guide covers every sprinkler tier, their exact coverage areas, proven layout patterns, and how to plan your own irrigation setup with our free Farm Layout Planner.

## Sprinkler Types and Coverage

There are three tiers of sprinklers in vanilla Stardew Valley, each with a different watering range.

### Basic Sprinkler
- **Unlock**: Farming Level 2
- **Crafting Cost**: 1 Copper Bar, 1 Iron Bar
- **Coverage**: Waters the 4 adjacent tiles directly up, down, left, and right of the sprinkler
- **Best For**: Tiny early-game gardens with a few crops

The Basic Sprinkler only waters four tiles, which makes it inefficient for larger plots. It is a stepping stone that helps you automate a small patch while you level up your Farming skill.

### Quality Sprinkler
- **Unlock**: Farming Level 6
- **Crafting Cost**: 1 Iron Bar, 1 Gold Bar, 1 Refined Quartz
- **Coverage**: Waters all 8 surrounding tiles in a 3×3 area, while the center tile (where the sprinkler sits) stays dry
- **Best For**: Mid-game crop fields of up to a few hundred tiles

The Quality Sprinkler is the workhorse of mid-game farming. It covers eight tiles per sprinkler, which is double the Basic Sprinkler's output for a modest increase in cost.

### Iridium Sprinkler
- **Unlock**: Farming Level 9
- **Crafting Cost**: 1 Iridium Bar, 1 Gold Bar, 1 Battery Pack
- **Coverage**: Waters all 24 surrounding tiles in a 5×5 area, with the center tile holding the sprinkler
- **Best For**: End-game mass production farms and giant crop layouts

The Iridium Sprinkler is the ultimate automation tool. One sprinkler covers an entire 5×5 block, making large-scale crop farming effortless. You can buy them from Krobus on Fridays for 10,000g, or find them in treasure chests and the Skull Cavern.

## Layout Patterns

Efficient layouts stack sprinklers so their coverage areas do not overlap, giving you the maximum number of watered tiles per sprinkler.

### Basic Sprinkler Layout
Place a Basic Sprinkler and plant four crops on its adjacent tiles. To scale up, leave a two-tile gap between sprinklers so each covers its own four tiles. This layout works only for very early-game survival farming.

### Quality Sprinkler Layout
The standard Quality Sprinkler pattern covers a 3×3 area. When you place multiple sprinklers, keep a gap so each covers a unique 3×3 zone. A common trick is to use a 3×3 plot per sprinkler and let the sprinklers sit in the center of each plot.

**Note**: Crops cannot grow on the tile occupied by the sprinkler itself, so plan around the center tile.

### Iridium Sprinkler Layout
The Iridium Sprinkler covers a 5×5 area. Place one sprinkler at the center of every 5×5 plot for zero wasted space. With a grid of Iridium Sprinklers, you can cover hundreds of tiles while only losing a handful to sprinkler placement.

**Pro tip**: Put a scarecrow at the edge of each block and use paths or flooring around sprinklers to keep your farm organized.

## Sprinklers and Greenhouse

The Greenhouse is the best place to use sprinklers, because crops inside never die between seasons. Place Quality or Iridium Sprinklers along the greenhouse soil, then plant Ancient Fruit or Starfruit for year-round passive profit.
