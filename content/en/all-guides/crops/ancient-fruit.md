The Ancient Fruit is a fruit crop in Stardew Valley. A very rare fruit that has survived since ancient times. It's extraordinarily sweet. It grows in Spring, Summer, Fall and is one of the most reliable ways to build early-season income, so it belongs in almost every farm plan.

## At a Glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Season | Spring, Summer, Fall |
| Growth time | 28 days then regrows every 7 days |
| Regrow | Yes (7 days) |
| Seed cost | Unknown |
| Sell price | 550g |
| Max quality | Iridium |
| Category | fruit |
| Trellis | No |
| Giant crop | No |
| Farming XP | 38 |

## How to Grow

Plant Ancient Seeds in tilled, watered soil at the start of the season. The crop takes **28 days** to mature and then produces again every 7 days after the first harvest. Water every morning (or set up a sprinkler) so the growth timer never pauses.

**Harvest strategy**: This is a multi-harvest crop — plan to keep it planted all season and collect repeatedly.

## Profit Analysis

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Net profit per plant | 550g |
| Harvests per season | ~1 |
| Approx. season profit per plant | 550g |

With the **Tiller** profession, crop sell prices increase by 10%, lifting the season profit per plant to about **605g**. Multiplied across a 12×8 field, this crop becomes a solid seasonal income source.

## Tips & Pairings

- **Artisan processing**: this crop can be processed into Wine, Jelly, Dried Fruit for even higher value.
- **Sprinklers**: pair it with a sprinkler layout in the [Farm Layout Planner](/farmlayou/) to automate watering from day one.
- **Quality**: with higher Farming skill and fertilizer, silver/gold/iridium quality harvests raise the sell price well above the base 550g.
- **Season end**: crops wither at season change — time plantings so the final harvest lands before the 28th.
