The Fairy Rose is a flower crop in Stardew Valley. An extremely rare flower with a powerful, sweet perfume. It grows in Fall and is one of the most reliable ways to build early-season income, so it belongs in almost every farm plan.

## At a Glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Season | Fall |
| Growth time | 12 days |
| Regrow | No |
| Seed cost | Pierre's (200g); JojaMart (250g) |
| Sell price | 290g |
| Max quality | Iridium |
| Category | flower |
| Trellis | No |
| Giant crop | No |
| Farming XP | 29 |

## How to Grow

Plant Fairy Seeds in tilled, watered soil at the start of the season. The crop takes **12 days** to mature. Water every morning (or set up a sprinkler) so the growth timer never pauses.

**Harvest strategy**: This is a single-harvest crop — replant after each harvest to keep the field productive.

## Profit Analysis

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Net profit per plant | 90g |
| Harvests per season | ~2 |
| Approx. season profit per plant | 180g |

With the **Tiller** profession, crop sell prices increase by 10%, lifting the season profit per plant to about **238g**. Multiplied across a 12×8 field, this crop becomes a solid seasonal income source.

## Tips & Pairings

- **Artisan processing**: this crop can be processed into Honey for even higher value.
- **Sprinklers**: pair it with a sprinkler layout in the [Farm Layout Planner](/farmlayou/) to automate watering from day one.
- **Quality**: with higher Farming skill and fertilizer, silver/gold/iridium quality harvests raise the sell price well above the base 290g.
- **Season end**: crops wither at season change — time plantings so the final harvest lands before the 28th.
