The Coffee Bean is a seed crop in Stardew Valley. It'll keep you buzzin'. Plant in spring or summer to harvest more beans. It grows in Spring, Summer and is one of the most reliable ways to build early-season income, so it belongs in almost every farm plan.

## At a Glance

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Season | Spring, Summer |
| Growth time | 10 days then regrows every 2 days |
| Regrow | Yes (2 days) |
| Seed cost | Traveling Cart (2500g) |
| Sell price | 15g |
| Max quality | Iridium |
| Category | seed |
| Trellis | No |
| Giant crop | No |
| Farming XP | 4 |

## How to Grow

Plant Coffee Bean in tilled, watered soil at the start of the season. The crop takes **10 days** to mature and then produces again every 2 days after the first harvest. Water every morning (or set up a sprinkler) so the growth timer never pauses.

**Harvest strategy**: This is a multi-harvest crop — plan to keep it planted all season and collect repeatedly.

## Profit Analysis

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Net profit per plant | -2485g |
| Harvests per season | ~10 |
| Approx. season profit per plant | -2350g |

With the **Tiller** profession, crop sell prices increase by 10%, lifting the season profit per plant to about **-2340g**. Multiplied across a 12×8 field, this crop becomes a solid seasonal income source.
