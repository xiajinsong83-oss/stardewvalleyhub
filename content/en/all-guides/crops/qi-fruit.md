# Qi Fruit Guide: How to Grow Qi Fruit in Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter (4-Day Growth, Profit & Tips)

![Qi Fruit](/images/crops/qi-fruit/crop.png)

The Qi Fruit is a fruit crop in Stardew Valley. A fruit imbued with the essence of Qi. Used to complete Qi's special orders. It grows in Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter and is one of the most reliable ways to build early-season income, so it belongs in almost every farm plan.

## How to Grow

Plant Qi Bean in tilled, watered soil at the start of the season. The crop takes **4 days** to mature. Water every morning (or set up a sprinkler) so the growth timer never pauses.

**Harvest strategy**: This is a single-harvest crop — replant after each harvest to keep the field productive.

## Profit Analysis

| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| Net profit per plant | 1g |
| Harvests per season | ~7 |
| Approx. season profit per plant | 7g |

With the **Tiller** profession, crop sell prices increase by 10%, lifting the season profit per plant to about **7g**. Multiplied across a 12×8 field, this crop becomes a solid seasonal income source.

## Tips & Pairings

- **Artisan processing**: this crop can be processed into Wine, Jelly, Dried Fruit for even higher value.
- **Sprinklers**: pair it with a sprinkler layout in the [Farm Layout Planner](/farmlayou/) to automate watering from day one.
- **Quality**: with higher Farming skill and fertilizer, silver/gold/iridium quality harvests raise the sell price well above the base 1g.
- **Season end**: crops wither at season change — time plantings so the final harvest lands before the 28th.
