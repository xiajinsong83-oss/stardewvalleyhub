## Collection Tips

- **Radioactive Ore** is part of the Museum collection — donate your first copy before selling extras.
- Mining deeper floors and cracking geodes are the fastest ways to find new pieces.
- Artifacts are also found by tilling artifact spots — keep a Hoe on hand in the Mines and at the Beach.
- Track donations with the [Museum Checklist](/museum-checklist/).
