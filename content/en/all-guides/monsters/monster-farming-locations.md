Farming monsters efficiently means knowing exactly where each type spawns and at what time of day. This guide lists the best location for every monster family, what drops to expect, and the fastest route through each area.

## The Big Four Areas

| Area | Unlocked | Monsters found |
|---|---|---|
| **The Mines** (Pelican Town) | Start of game | Slimes, bugs, bats, ghosts, skeletons, duggies |
| **Skull Cavern** (Desert) | After Desert unlocked | Serpents, slimes, bats, dinosaurs, rock golems |
| **Volcano Dungeon** (Ginger Island) | After Island unlocked | Fire sprites, hot heads, sludges, tiger slimes |
| **Adventurer's Guild Quest Board** | Start of game | Targeted bounties for specific monsters |

## Slimes

| Slime type | Location | Drop |
|---|---|---|
| Green Slime | Mines floors 5–40 | Slime, Slime Eggs |
| Blue Slime | Mines floors 40–80 | Slime |
| Red Slime | Mines floors 80–120 | Slime, Slime Eggs |
| Purple Slime | Skull Cavern any floor | Slime, Prismatic Shard (rare) |
| Tiger Slime | Volcano Dungeon, Ginger Island | Tiger Slime Egg, Slime |
| Big Slime (event) | Slime Hut after construction | Massive slime drops |

**Fastest farm**: Build the Slime Hut, fill it with 20+ slimes, and harvest daily. This is passive monster farming — no combat required.

## Bats

| Bat type | Location | Drop |
|---|---|---|
| Bat | Mines floors 5–30 | Bat Wing |
| Frost Bat | Mines floors 40–80 | Bat Wing |
| Lava Bat | Mines floors 80–120 | Bat Wing, Solar Quartz |
| Iridium Bat | Skull Cavern (rare, deep) | Bat Wing, Iridium Ore |

**Fastest farm**: Push to level 80+ in the regular Mines and sweep a floor. Lava Bats cluster in groups.

## Skeletons & Ghosts

- **Skeletons**: Mines floors 70–120. Drop: Bone Fragments (used for crafting), Archaeological Fragments.
- **Ghosts**: Mines floors 50–90. Drop: Ghost Crystals (sell price 50g), Ectoplasm.

**Tip**: Ghosts float through walls — don't chase them into corners. Use a sword with knockback to keep distance.

## Duggies (Sand Diggers)

- **Location**: Mines floors 40–80 (sand patches) and Skull Cavern.
- **Drop**: Quartz, Earth Crystal.
- **Tactic**: They burst out of sand. Walk slowly over sand tiles and swing as they pop up.

## Serpents (Skull Cavern)

- **Location**: Skull Cavern, any floor (more common deeper).
- **Drop**: Solar Core, Battery Pack.
- **Tactic**: They fly fast and do heavy damage. Use a Galaxy Sword and stay moving. Don't let them hit you head-on.

## Pepper Rex (Dinosaurs)

- **Location**: Skull Cavern, rare.
- **Drop**: Dinosaur Mayo (if you have a dino egg into the Mayo machine), Dinosaur Egg.
- **Tactic**: Big knockback hammer is your friend. Hit and retreat.

## Rock Golems

- **Location**: Skull Cavern (look like stone piles until approached).
- **Drop**: Stone, Geodes.
- **Tactic**: They're slow but hit hard. Circle-strafe and swing from behind.

## Fire Sprites & Hot Heads (Volcano)

- **Location**: Volcano Dungeon floors 1–10+.
- **Drop**: Flame Ore, Magma Cap.
- **Tactic**: Bring water or wait for them to cool. Hot Heads explode on death — keep distance.

## Recommended Routes

| Goal | Best route |
|---|---|
| Slime farming (passive) | Build Slime Hut, harvest daily |
| Bat Wings | Mines floor 80–90 sweep |
| Bone Fragments | Mines floor 70–100 skeleton floor |
| Iridium Ore | Skull Cavern deep run with Coffee + Spicy Eel |
| Solar Cores | Skull Cavern serpent run |
| Dinosaur Eggs | Skull Cavern lucky deep run |

## Prep Before a Farming Run

1. **Food**: Spicy Eel (+ luck, speed) or Lucky Lunch.
2. **Buffs**: Coffee (speed), Crab Cakes (luck).
3. **Gear**: Galaxy/Infinity Sword, Slingshot with Explosive Ammo.
4. **Totem**: Desert Warp for Skull Cavern, Farm Warp for quick exits.
