The Blue Discus is a regular fish in Stardew Valley. A brightly colored tropical fish that is popular in aquariums.

## Catch Conditions

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Season | Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter |
| Time | 6:00 AM – 2:00 AM |
| Weather | both |
| Location | Ginger Island (freshwater) |
| Difficulty | 60 / 100 |
| Sell price | 120g |
| Catch type | rod |

## How to Catch

Go to **Ginger Island (freshwater)** during the right season and time window. The Blue Discus is Moderate — an upgraded rod and bait make it reliable..

**Bait & tackle**: use Bait (or Magnet/Lure) and an upgraded rod to shrink the catch window. For hard fish, hold the bobber high and tap, don't hold — release pressure the instant the bar drops.

## Uses & Tips

- **Sell value**: base price **120g** — with the Fisher/Angler professions it sells for **150g**.
- **Cooking**: used in Maki Roll, Quality Fertilizer, Sashimi.
- **Smoking**: can be smoked in a Fish Smoker for extra value.
- **Fish Pond**: keeps in a Fish Pond and produces Roe/items at population milestones.
