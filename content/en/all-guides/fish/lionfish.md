The Lionfish is a regular fish in Stardew Valley. An aggressive, predatory fish with venomous spines.

## Catch Conditions

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| Season | Spring, Summer, Fall, Winter |
| Time | 6:00 AM – 2:00 AM |
| Weather | both |
| Location | Ginger Island |
| Difficulty | 50 / 100 |
| Sell price | 100g |
| Catch type | rod |

## How to Catch

Go to **Ginger Island** during the right season and time window. The Lionfish is Moderate — an upgraded rod and bait make it reliable..

**Bait & tackle**: use Bait (or Magnet/Lure) and an upgraded rod to shrink the catch window. For hard fish, hold the bobber high and tap, don't hold — release pressure the instant the bar drops.

## Uses & Tips

- **Sell value**: base price **100g** — with the Fisher/Angler professions it sells for **125g**.
- **Cooking**: used in Maki Roll, Quality Fertilizer, Sashimi.
- **Smoking**: can be smoked in a Fish Smoker for extra value.
- **Fish Pond**: keeps in a Fish Pond and produces Roe/items at population milestones.
