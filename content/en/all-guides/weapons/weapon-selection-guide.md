Picking the right weapon matters far more than raw damage numbers. Each weapon class fits a different job: swords clear packs fast, daggers melt single targets, hammers knock crowds back, and slingshots let you fight from safety. This guide walks you through which weapon to use for each activity in Stardew Valley.

## The Four Weapon Classes

| Class | Strengths | Best for |
|---|---|---|
| **Swords** | Wide arc, balanced damage, knockback | General Mines diving, mixed packs |
| **Daggers** | Fast swing, low per-hit damage | Slime Hut, farming weak enemies |
| **Hammers** | Slow, high damage, strong knockback | Skull Cavern tanking, brutes/dinos |
| **Slingshots** | Ranged, ammo costs | Safe farming, flying enemies |

## Early Game (Spring–Year 1)

You start with a **Wooden Sword** from the Adventurer's Guild. Don't upgrade it early — save your copper.

- **Mines 1–40**: Steel Smallsword (bought at the Guild for 1,000g) is enough. Clear green slimes and flying bugs.
- **Mines 40–60**: Grab the **Lava Katana**? No — that's late. Instead, pick up the **Obsidian Edge** on level 90. Before that, use the **Burglars' Shank** if you find it in chests.
- **Tactic**: Run in circles, swing once, step back. Don't trade hits.

## Mid Game (Year 1 Summer–Fall)

Once you unlock the **Skull Cavern** (after the Desert), weapon choice changes.

- **Recommended loadout**: Galaxy Sword (obtained by inserting a Prismatic Shard into the pillar in the desert) + a Slingshot as backup.
- **Galaxy Sword** is the first weapon that trivializes most combat. Get it as soon as you have a Prismatic Shard — don't waste shards on crafting until you have it.
- **Slingshot + Explosive Ammo**: For flying enemies that hover out of melee range, stand still and fire. It's safer than chasing them down.

## Late Game (Year 2+)

- **Infinity Blade**: Combine the Galaxy Sword with 3 Galaxy Souls (bought from the老 dwarf in the Volcano Dungeon) at the same desert pillar. This is the final sword — no need to chase anything else.
- **For Slime Hut**: Use a **Dagger** (Galaxy Dagger or Infinity Dagger). Fast swings melt slimes before they multiply.
- **For Volcano Dungeon**: Hammers feel slow here. Stick to Infinity Blade or an enchanted Galaxy Hammer if you like knockback.

## Quick Recommendations by Activity

| Activity | Pick |
|---|---|
| Regular Mines (1–120) | Any sword with +damage enchant |
| Skull Cavern speedrunning | Galaxy/Infinity Sword + Slingshot |
| Slime Hut farming | Dagger (fast swing) |
| Volcano Dungeon | Infinity Blade |
| Farming flying bugs in the Mines | Slingshot with basic stones |
| Qi Challenges (high HP enemies) | Hammer + knockback enchant |

## Upgrading Priority

1. **Galaxy Sword** (Prismatic Shard → desert pillar) — this is your power spike.
2. **Galaxy Dagger** (same method, choose dagger) — for Slime Hut.
3. **Infinity Blade** (3 Galaxy Souls) — endgame.
4. Enchantments (Haymaker, Vampiric, etc.) come last; they're gravy, not the main event.

## Common Mistakes

- **Wasting Prismatic Shards** on clothing or crafting before you have the Galaxy Sword. The Sword power spike outweighs everything else.
- **Using a dagger in the Mines** — you'll get swarmed because your per-hit damage is too low.
- **Ignoring the Slingshot** — it's not just a toy. Flying enemies are dangerous up close, and the Slingshot lets you farm them safely from across the room.
- **Upgrading the Wooden Sword** — it's a trap. Save resources for the Galaxy step.
